Step 4

What changed

  • The real logo, everywhere. The new hammer Atlas wordmark replaces typed text, and titles use the game's own trailer font.
  • Real gameplay everywhere. New 1080p clips recorded straight from the game (no HUD), clips from the trailer, the full trailer in a player, and real card renders.
  • Steam-first. “Wishlist on Steam” (classic Steam blue) is the main button everywhere, with “Join the beta on Discord” right beside it. The Steam button goes live the moment we have the store link.
  • One look across every page. Shared nav and footer, painted key-art headers, and socials (Discord, X, Instagram, TikTok) on every page.
  • Mobile and accessibility pass. Phone layouts, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.
Heads-up

Placeholders & notes

  • The trailer is the current cut, served from this preview. It moves to YouTube once uploaded.
  • The Steam buttons don't go anywhere yet; they light up once the store page is live.
  • Press: Matan's kit and screenshots are untouched. The page was restyled and got a trailer section and an “About the game” blurb, so please sanity-check those.
  • This preview has its own address and is hidden from search engines. The real domain is untouched.
Step 5

Coming next: analytics, reimagined

Turning the stats into something people come back to and share, with honest numbers (winrates with confidence ranges, no fake precision):

  • State of the Meta front page: what changed this month, how every commander is doing, the few real counters, and fun “how games go” facts.
  • A profile page for every commander covering how it wins, matchups, ways to play (open in the deck builder), signature cards, and an opening-hand guide.
  • “What beats X?” matchup explorer, an art-first Cards library, and a searchable Community Decks library (copy any deck code).
  • An Archive tab that keeps every old page reachable.
