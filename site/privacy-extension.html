Privacy Policy

Twitch extension · Last updated 2 September 2026

This page covers the Atlas Conquest Twitch extension. Looking for the game itself? Atlas Conquest game privacy policy.

This describes what the Atlas Conquest Twitch extension collects. For viewers, the answer is nothing.

If you are watching a stream

The extension collects no information about you at all. It has no account, sets no cookies, and stores nothing about who you are or what you hover.

What it does is receive a description of the streamer's game board so it can show you the cards. That description contains only what is already visible on the stream: which cards are on the board, where they are, and their current health. It is sent by the streamer's own game.

Card images and card text are downloaded from cdn.atlas-conquest.com, which is static file hosting. Those requests carry the same information any web request does, such as your IP address, and are not used to build a profile of you.

If you are a streamer

When you link your Twitch channel in the game, we receive your Twitch channel name and numeric channel id, and store a token that lets the game publish to that one channel. We do not receive your Twitch password, your email address, or access to your account beyond identifying which channel authorised the link.

While you play, the game sends your board state so viewers can hover it. This happens only while your channel is live and only while the feature is switched on. Your opponent's hidden information is never included, because the game itself never receives it.

Board state is relayed, not stored. It passes through our service to Twitch and is not written to a database or retained afterwards.

You can stop publishing at any time in Settings → Twitch. Unlinking removes the stored token.

Settings you change on Twitch

Your extension settings, such as the stream delay, are stored by Twitch in their own configuration service. They never reach our servers.

Who else is involved

  • Twitch — delivers extension messages to viewers and stores your settings.
  • Cloudflare — runs the service that relays board state to Twitch.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the card images.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone, including them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will change.
