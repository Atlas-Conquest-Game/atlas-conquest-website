Gameplay

1v116 commanders6 patron gods280+ cards

  • Play Cards On The Battlefield

    Play minions onto tiles you’ve claimed, or cast spells for one-shot effects.

  • Expand Your Territory

    Claim tiles to grow your land. Each pays 1 mana a turn; villages pay 2.

  • Command Your Army

    March across the hexes and attack. If the defender falls, you take its tile.

  • Conquer Your Opponents

    Defeat the enemy commander to win. Every match is one on one, online.

Three Battlefields

  • Dunes

    Desert ruins

  • Snowmelt

    Frozen glacier
