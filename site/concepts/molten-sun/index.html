Five Beats of Battle

One on one, turn by turn, on a hex map. Every match moves through the same five beats — how you play them is up to you.

  1. I

    Play Cards on the Battlefield

    Minions arrive on tiles inside your territory and fight as units on the hex board. Spells strike once — then they're gone.

  2. II

    Expand Your Territory

    March your commander out to claim tiles. Each pays a mana next turn — villages pay two — and Dominion caps how much land you hold.

  3. III

    Command Your Army

    Move by Speed, attack by marching into an enemy. Both sides strike at once — if the defender falls, the attacker takes its tile.

  4. IV

    Upgrade Your Deck

    280+ cards, 16 commanders. Build 40–60 card decks from your patron's cards and the neutral pool.

  5. V

    Conquer Your Opponents

    Bring the enemy commander's health to zero and the match is yours. Simple to say. Getting there is the game.
