I

Chapter I

Play Cards On The Battlefield

Your hand is your army. Minions land on the hex grid as units with Power, Speed and Health. Spells strike once, with range and radius, then they’re gone.

  • Minions take the field
  • Spells strike, then vanish
  • Some cards trigger on arrival
II

Chapter II

Expand Your Territory

Claim hexes to push your borders outward. Every tile you hold pays mana each turn, villages pay double, and minions can only enter play inside your territory, unless they have Deploy.

  • Tiles pay mana
  • Villages pay double
  • Play only on your land
III

Chapter III

Command Your Army

March your commander and minions across the grid, then strike. Both sides deal their Power at once, damage stays, and when a defender falls the attacker takes its tile.

  • Both sides strike at once
  • Wounds carry over
  • Win the fight, take the hex
IV

Chapter IV

Upgrade Your Deck

Pick a commander, then build the deck it leads, and keep tuning it between battles.

  1. 280+ CardsSearch, filter and sort the whole card pool.
  2. 16+ CommandersEach one serves a patron god and brings its own stats.
  3. Unlimited Deckbuilding Potential40–60 cards, up to 3 copies each: your patron’s cards plus neutrals.
V

Chapter V

Conquer Your Opponents

Face another player online, one on one. Break their lines, bring the enemy commander’s Health to zero, and the battlefield is yours.

  • Online 1v1
  • Dunes · Snowmelt · Tropics
  • Topple their commander
