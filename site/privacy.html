Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Atlas Conquest is a multiplayer card game, and this policy covers both the game and the Atlas Conquest Twitch extension. It describes what is collected, why, and how to stop it.

The short version

  • The Twitch extension collects nothing from viewers. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking.
  • The game collects gameplay statistics, and you can switch that off in Settings.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

The Twitch extension

If you are watching a stream, the extension collects no information about you at all. It has no account, sets no cookies, and stores nothing about who you are or what you hover.

What it does is receive a description of the streamer's game board so it can show you the cards. That description contains only what is already visible on the stream: which cards are on the board, where they are, and their current health. It is sent by the streamer's own game.

Card images and card text are downloaded from cdn.atlas-conquest.com, which is static file hosting. Those requests carry the usual information any web request does, such as your IP address, and are not used to build a profile of you.

If you are a streamer

When you link your Twitch channel in the game, we receive your Twitch channel name and numeric channel id, and store a token that lets the game publish to that one channel. We do not receive your Twitch password, your email address, or access to your account beyond identifying which channel authorised the link.

While you play, the game sends your board state so viewers can hover it. This happens only while your channel is live and only while the feature is switched on. Your opponent's hidden information is never included, because the game itself never receives it. You can stop publishing at any time in Settings → Twitch, and unlinking removes the stored token.

Board state is relayed, not stored. It passes through our service to Twitch and is not written to a database or retained afterwards.

The game

Accounts

Signing in uses Google or Discord through Unity Authentication. We receive an identifier for your account, not your password. Sign-in is used to save your decks and match your games.

Gameplay analytics

With your consent, the game records gameplay events through Unity Gaming Services: matches started and finished, their length and outcome, the commander and deck used, menus opened, and any feedback you choose to submit. This exists to balance cards and find where the game confuses people. It does not include chat, personal messages, or anything you type other than feedback you deliberately send.

Consent, and how to turn it off

In the EU, EEA and UK, analytics are off until you opt in. Elsewhere they are on by default and you may opt out at any time. Either way the control is the same: Settings → Analytics. Turning it off stops collection and deletes what was previously collected.

To apply that rule we need to know which country you are in, so on first launch the game looks up the country associated with your IP address using ipapi.co. Only the two-letter country code is kept, stored locally on your machine, and the lookup is not repeated.

Who else is involved

  • Unity Gaming Services — accounts, multiplayer, and analytics.
  • Twitch — delivers extension messages to viewers and stores streamer settings.
  • Cloudflare — runs the service that relays board state to Twitch.
  • GitHub Pages — serves card images and this website.
  • ipapi.co — one country lookup on first launch.

Children

Atlas Conquest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and it will be removed.

Your choices

  • Turn analytics off in Settings → Analytics, which also deletes what was collected.
  • Unlink Twitch in Settings → Twitch, which stops publishing and removes the token.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or its deletion, using the contact address below.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the game will say so on next launch.
