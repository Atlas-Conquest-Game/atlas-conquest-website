Description

In Atlas Conquest, you assemble a deck of cards that become units on a physical battlefield. Play as a commander, leading armies on behalf of a patron god. Battle real players in online competitive matches. Outmaneuver your rival, and conquer the map!

Features

  • Cards become units that move and fight on a hex-grid battlefield.
  • 16 commanders to lead your army, each with their own abilities.
  • 287 cards across six patron gods.
  • Three maps to conquer: Dunes, Snowmelt and Tropics.
  • Online competitive matches against real players.
  • Build decks in game or on the web, and share them as deck codes.
