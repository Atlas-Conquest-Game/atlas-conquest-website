Where these numbers come from

Every stat here is computed from matches the game server recorded during the Atlas Conquest beta and earlier playtests, and the numbers refresh once a day. The player base is still small, so samples can be thin: rates built on very few games are hidden, and game counts sit next to the rest so you can judge for yourself. Play in the beta and your matches land here too.
