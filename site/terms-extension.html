These terms cover the Atlas Conquest Twitch extension. Installing or using it means you accept them. Twitch's own terms apply as well.

What the extension does

It shows viewers the cards on a streamer's board. Hovering a character displays that card at full size; clicking keeps it open.

For streamers

Linking your channel in Atlas Conquest authorises the game to publish your board state to that channel while you are live. Only your own visible game information is published. You can stop at any time in Settings → Twitch.

A note on competitive play

Publishing your board makes it easier for viewers to follow, and equally easier for an opponent watching your stream. Whether that is acceptable is between you and whoever runs your tournament. The switch exists for exactly this reason.

For viewers

The extension is for reading cards. Do not attempt to interfere with it, extract data from it in bulk, or use it to gain an advantage in a match you are playing.
