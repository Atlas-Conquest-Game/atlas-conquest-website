Atlas Conquest is a competitive deck builder fought on a hex-grid map. You lead a **commander**, summon minions from a 40-card deck, claim territory to grow your mana, and win by reducing the enemy commander's health to zero. A match takes about 15–25 minutes.

This guide takes you from installing the game to finishing your first match. For the full rulebook, see the [Detailed Rules](/articles/detailed-rules/) reference.

## Getting the Game

Atlas Conquest is in alpha, and builds are distributed through our community:

1. **[Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/7QaEY4yJH5)** — the game is free.
2. Grab the latest build from the pinned messages and unzip it anywhere.
3. Launch, pick a name, and use **Quick Join** to find an opponent — or arrange a match in Discord (there's almost always someone up for teaching a new player).

## The Goal

**Defeat the enemy commander.** Your commander is a character on the board with a health total (usually around 30). When an enemy commander's health hits zero, the game ends immediately. Everything else — minions, territory, mana — exists to make that happen (or stop it happening to you).

## The Board

![A mid-game battle on the Tropics map](board.jpg)

Matches are played on one of three maps — **Dunes**, **Snowmelt**, and **Tropics** — each a field of hexagonal tiles:

- **Normal tiles** produce **1 mana** per turn while you have them claimed.
- **Villages** produce **2 mana** — they're worth fighting over.
- **Mountains** block movement unless a character has *Flying*.

Your claimed territory is tinted in your color, the enemy's in theirs. Territory is your economy: the more tiles you hold, the more mana you get at the start of each of your turns, and the bigger the plays you can make.

## Your Commander

Your commander starts on the board and defines your whole run:

| Stat | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Health** | Damage you can take before losing the game |
| **Speed** | Tiles your commander moves per turn |
| **Dominion** | Maximum tiles you can have claimed at once |
| **Intellect** | Maximum hand size — and the size of your opening-hand choice |

Every commander also has a **patron god** that determines which cards your deck may contain (plus neutral cards), and a special **Claim** ability: once per turn, your commander can claim the tile they're standing on. Early game, your commander is a settler — walk, claim, repeat.

![The patron gods](patron-gods.png)

Pick a **starter deck** for your first games — each is built around one commander and one straightforward gameplan (goblin rush, beast synergy, paladin defense, dark aggression, or spell control).

## Your First Match, Turn by Turn

### The mulligan

You'll be shown a set of cards from your deck (as many as your commander's Intellect). Keep exactly **3** if you're going first, **4** if you're going second — the rest shuffle back. Keep cheap cards: things you can actually play in the first few turns, like [[card:Baby Raptor]] or [[card:Goblin Infantry]]. A hand full of expensive bombs feels great and does nothing for five turns.

### The start of each turn

Automatically, you: **draw a card**, **refill your mana** (equal to your claimed tiles' production), and your characters **recover their movement and attack**.

### Things you can do on your turn

- **Move characters** — drag them; each can move up to its Speed in tiles.
- **Claim territory** — your commander's Claim ability, once per turn, on the tile they occupy.
- **Play minions** — drag a card from your hand onto a tile **inside your territory** (cards with *Deploy* break this rule). The minion arrives unable to act this turn unless it has *Haste*.
- **Cast spells** — one-shot effects like [[card:Fireball]]; drag onto a legal target.
- **Battle** — drag a character onto an adjacent enemy. Both deal damage equal to their **Power** to each other's **Health**. If the defender dies, the attacker steps into their tile. Each character gets one attack per turn, and damage sticks between turns.
- **End your turn** — if you're over your Intellect in cards, you'll discard down first.

### A sensible opening (turns 1–4)

1. **Move your commander and claim every turn.** Territory compounds — a tile claimed on turn 1 pays mana all game. Head toward a village if one is near.
2. **Play something cheap early** — a [[card:Pack Wolf]] or [[card:Emerald Asp]] on the board beats a better card in your hand.
3. **Screen your commander.** Keep a minion between your commander and the shortest path from the enemy's side.
4. **Don't feed minions into bad trades.** Check the numbers: attacking a 3-power enemy with a 2-health minion is a funeral, not a battle.

### The midgame

Around turns 5–8, mana curves peak and armies collide — usually over villages and chokepoints (mountains funnel movement, and a well-placed minion can wall a pass). Spend your mana every turn if you can; unspent mana is a turn you partially skipped. When you hit your **Dominion** cap, a gray flag appears by your mana counter — drag it onto a low-value tile to unclaim it, then claim something better.

### Closing it out

Chip damage wins games. Commanders rarely fight back (most have 0 power), so once your force is bigger, push at the commander directly rather than grinding every minion off the board. Abilities that reach — *Range*, spells like [[card:Lightning Bolt]] — finish what your minions start.

## Reading Cards

![Card anatomy — cost, stats, and text box](card-text.png)

Hover any card in game (or any [[card:Stout Knight]]-style link on this site) to see it in full. The keywords you'll meet first:

- **Arrival** — does something when played.
- **Haste** — can move and attack the turn it's played.
- **Deploy** — can be played outside your territory.
- **Trample** — kills refund the attack; can keep battling.
- **Legendary** — only one copy on the board at a time.
- **Cooldown N** — the ability needs *N* turns to recharge.

The full keyword list lives in the [Detailed Rules](/articles/detailed-rules/).
